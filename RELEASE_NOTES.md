# PBB PreAudit v1.0.0

- Uses the supplied Pre-Audit PBB logo and native Windows/macOS icons.
- Marks confirmed name/TIN identities as Pre-audited; service eligibility remains a separate result.
- Saves service reviews independently of identity matching, including roster-rule retirees.
- Preserves manual matches, rejected candidates, and notes when service reviews change.
- Keeps conflicting historical TINs, invalid amounts, incomplete records, and ambiguous names in review.
- Includes monthly findings, proration details, right-click matching, next-entry review, and CSV export and an Excel workbook grouped into IUs, SDO, and School-based tabs.
- Uses short CSV remarks, with full explanations available in the result window.

Download the Windows setup EXE or the DMG matching your Mac. Run PBB-PreAudit-Setup-1.0.0.exe to install the Windows app and its shortcuts. On macOS, open the DMG and drag PBB PreAudit.app to Applications; Intel and Apple Silicon builds are separate.

These builds are unsigned and the macOS app is not Apple notarized. macOS may require approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security. No payroll files, PSIPOP records, TIN reference files, or saved review decisions are included. Pre-audit results are service-based checks; remaining memorandum conditions still require validation.
