How to use the results

Calculations use each workbook’s embedded salary table, service factors, and agency rate. Personnel differences and possible separation matches need supporting-record review. A separation in 2025 does not establish ineligibility for 2024. These checks do not determine final eligibility.

Excel formulas use their last saved results. Recalculate and save in Excel before auditing if values are missing or stale. Correct the source files in Excel, then rerun the audit.
